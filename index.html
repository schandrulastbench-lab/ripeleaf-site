Plant care that keeps you calm.

Gentle reminders, a diary for every plant, and nothing watching you. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Your garden, sorted. Today shows what needs water, feed or a quick check, grouped by room. Tick a whole room off in one go, or mark plants done straight from your lock screen.

A diary, not a database. Photos, notes and every watering build into a timeline you can look back on. Hundreds of species with care guides and pet-safety notes, written for Australian homes.

Private by design. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers. Your plants live on your iPhone and, if you like, in your own iCloud. We can't see any of it.

Pay once. Free for your first three plants. A one-time unlock adds as many as you like — no subscription needed.
