Plant care that keeps you calm.

Gentle reminders, a diary for every plant, and nothing watching you. Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Your garden, sorted. Today shows what needs water, feed or a quick check, grouped by room. Tick a whole room off in one go, or mark a plant done straight from its reminder.

A diary, not a database. Photos, notes and every watering build into a timeline you can look back on. Over 1,000 species with care guides and pet-safety notes, written with Australian homes in mind.

Private by design. No account, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers. Your plants live on your device and, if you like, in your own iCloud. We have no way to see any of it.

Pay once. Free to download, with up to three plants. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks as many as you like. It's not a subscription.

Care guides and pet-safety notes are general information, not professional or veterinary advice.
