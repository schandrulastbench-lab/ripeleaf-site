Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy is written in plain language. If anything is unclear, email support@ripeleaf.app.

The short version: RipeLeaf doesn't collect your personal data. Your plants, photos, notes and reminders stay on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud account. We can't see, access or share any of it. There's no RipeLeaf account, no sign-in, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

RipeLeaf is an iPhone plant-care app made by an independent developer in Australia ("we", "us"). Contact: support@ripeleaf.app.

What the app stores, and where

On your iPhone

RipeLeaf stores everything locally using Apple's on-device database: your plant names, species and care schedules; photos you take or add; care history, notes and diary entries; and your app settings. A small summary of today's tasks is also shared on your device with the RipeLeaf home-screen widget. None of this is sent to us.

In your own iCloud (optional)

If iCloud is on, RipeLeaf syncs your data across your Apple devices using Apple's private CloudKit service. It's stored in your iCloud account, not on any server of ours, and we have no access to it. Apple looks after its security under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn this off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

RipeLeaf doesn't run any server or backend that receives your personal data.

Camera and photos

With your permission, RipeLeaf uses the camera so you can photograph your plants, and you can also add photos from your library. Photos are kept on your device (and in your iCloud if sync is on). We never upload, analyse or receive your photos.

Location and weather

If you allow it, RipeLeaf uses your approximate location to show today's local weather and tailor care tips. To do this, your device sends that location to Apple's WeatherKit service, which returns the forecast. The location isn't stored by RipeLeaf and is never sent to us. Location is optional — without it the app works fully and shows seasonal tips instead. Change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → RipeLeaf.

Notifications

Care reminders (water, feed, rotate, check on) are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS itself. They don't come from a server and don't send any data off your device. Manage them in Settings → Notifications → RipeLeaf.

Exporting your garden

You can export all your plants, history and photos as a file. It's created on your device, and it only goes wherever you choose to send or save it.

Purchases and ratings

RipeLeaf offers a one-time unlock. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see your card or payment details. Occasionally, at a good moment, the app may show Apple's standard "rate this app" prompt; any rating or review goes to Apple and the App Store, not to us.

Tracking and analytics

RipeLeaf does not track you across apps or websites, does not include any third-party analytics or advertising code, does not share data with data brokers or advertisers, and does not build a profile of you. There's nothing to opt out of, because nothing is running.

What we can access

To be completely direct: we cannot access your personal data. We have no server that receives it and no way to retrieve it from your device or your iCloud. If you email us, we'll receive whatever you choose to put in that email, and we'll use it only to reply to you.

Deleting your data

Delete a plant to remove it with its photos and history. Delete the app to remove everything from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → RipeLeaf.

Children

RipeLeaf is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children — or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If RipeLeaf changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, a future optional account feature — we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date before that change ships.

Contact

Email support@ripeleaf.app. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
