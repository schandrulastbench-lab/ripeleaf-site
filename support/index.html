Common questions

My reminders aren't showing up

Check Settings → Notifications → RipeLeaf and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open RipeLeaf once — it reschedules your reminders each time it opens. In the app, You → Reminders sets the time of day and any quiet days.

I bought the unlock but it's asking me to pay again

Go to You → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. The unlock is a one-time purchase and is yours for good on all your devices.

My plants aren't syncing between my iPhone and iPad

Sync uses your own iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID, iCloud is on (Settings → [your name] → iCloud), and there's an internet connection. You → About shows the current iCloud status. A first sync can take a few minutes.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. You → About → Export your garden creates a zip with all your plants, care history and photos, which you can save to Files or send anywhere.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting a plant removes it and its photos and history. Deleting the app removes everything from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → RipeLeaf.

Is the pet-safety information reliable?

It's a general guide, cross-checked against sources such as the ASPCA's plant lists, and we lean towards "toxic" when information is thin. It isn't veterinary advice — if your pet has eaten a plant, contact your vet straight away.

My plant isn't in the list

You can add it under any name you like and set the watering and feeding yourself. Tell us which plant it was and we'll look at adding it.
